HOW TO READ THIS

A first screen is a starting point.

MET

Residential or potentially residential zoning code is present in the official layer.

CONFLICT

Commercial, industrial, agricultural or open-space coding needs a closer land-use review.

UNKNOWN

The parcel record does not include a usable zoning code. Verify with the city or county.

Site Scout is an early research aid, not a zoning determination, title report, survey, environmental review, or legal opinion. Confirm every item with the jurisdiction and qualified professionals.
